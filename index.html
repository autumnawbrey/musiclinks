II.   Access Points
 Spotify →
Wave equation
ψ(x, t) = A sin(kx − ωt) (1)
where ψ = displacement · A = amplitude · k = wavenumber · x = position · ω = angular frequency · t = time
 Apple Music →
Fourier decomposition
ℱ{f}(ξ) = ∫ f(t) e−2πiξt dt (2)
where ℱ = Fourier operator · f(t) = time-domain signal · ξ = frequency variable · i = imaginary unit · t = time
 TikTok →
Logistic growth
N(t) = K / (1 + e−r(t−t0)) (3)
where N(t) = spread at time t · K = maximum reach · r = growth rate · t0 = time of maximum growth rate
 Instagram →
Laplacian of an image
∇2I(x,y) = 0 (4)
where ∇2 = Laplacian operator · I(x,y) = image intensity at pixel (x,y) · 0 = smooth uniform region
 Tidal →
Sound intensity
I = 2π2ν2δ2ρc (5)
where I = intensity · ν = frequency · δ = displacement amplitude · ρ = medium density · c = speed of sound
 YouTube Music →
Exponential growth
N(t) = N0 · ert (6)
where N(t) = count at time t · N₀ = initial count · r = growth rate · e = Euler's number · t = time
 Amazon Music →
Total energy of a wave
Etotal = ½μA2ω2λ (7)
where A = amplitude · ω = angular frequency · λ = wavelength · μ = linear mass density
 Contact →
Direct channel with zero information loss
P(received  |  sent) = 1 (8)
where P = probability · 1 = certainty
